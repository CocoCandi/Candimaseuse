02 — Benefits

What a session does for you.

A good massage isn't just a luxury. It's one of the simplest ways to look after a hardworking body and a busy mind.

Melts away pain & tightness

Hours at a desk, in traffic or on your feet lock tension into your neck, shoulders and lower back. Focused hands-on work releases those knots so you move freely again.

Helps you sleep deeper

When your muscles finally let go, your nervous system slows down. Many clients drift into deep, uninterrupted sleep after a session and wake properly rested.

Boosts circulation & energy

Steady strokes get fresh blood and oxygen moving through tired muscles, clearing that heavy, sluggish feeling and leaving your body lighter.

Clears mental stress

Stepping away from your phone and daily noise for 60 to 90 minutes gives your mind room to breathe, easing everyday anxiety and lifting your mood.

05 — Questions

Good to know.

Do you offer massage at home and in hotels in Benin City?

Yes. Candimaseuse Spa is a mobile massage service. We come to your private home or hotel anywhere in Benin City, including GRA, Ihama, Airport Road, Ugbor, Sapele Road and Ugbowo, and surrounding neighbourhoods.

What massage sessions do you offer?

Full Body Massage (deep tissue or Swedish), Hot Stone Therapy and Aromatherapy Massage are 60 minutes each. The Premium Experience combines all three in 90 minutes. Custom and extended sessions are available on request.

What are your opening hours?

We work from 6:00 AM to 7:00 PM daily. Late-evening and overnight sessions are available when you book ahead.

How much does a massage cost?

We share current session rates on WhatsApp when you book. Send your preferred session, date and area through the booking form and we will confirm availability and the rate.
